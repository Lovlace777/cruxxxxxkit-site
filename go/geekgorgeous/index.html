Pros / 核心优势

  • One-step conversion to retinoic acid delivers visible firmness and texture smoothing in 6–8 weeks
  • Silky yellow emulsion buffered with Allantoin, Panthenol, and Biosaccharide Gum-1 prevents retinoid flaking
  • Costs ~$20 vs $75+ for comparable 0.1% retinaldehyde serums from luxury derm brands
  • 离维A酸仅一步之遥（A醛 -> A酸），比普通A醇（A醇 -> A醛 -> A酸）刺激度更低、抗老紧致见效快一倍
  • 复配尿囊素、泛醇与生物糖胶-1，黄色丝滑乳液质地温和不拔干
  • 真空避光按压瓶确保每一滴 0.1% 视黄醛直到用空都保持最高活性
